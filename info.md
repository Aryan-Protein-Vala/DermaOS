# DermaOS — Complete App Overview

> **"Skincare is Biology, Not Marketing"**
> The first skincare marketplace that filters by YOUR DNA.

---

## 🎯 What is DermaOS?

DermaOS is a personalized skincare platform that grades 14,000+ products specifically for each user's unique skin biology. Instead of generic product reviews, users get A-F grades based on their skin type, concerns, and sensitivities.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 1. **AI Skin Scanner** (Premium)
- Upload selfie or use camera
- AI analyzes skin type, sensitivity, concerns
- Generates personalized skin profile
- One-time scan: ₹399 / $4.99
- Unlimited scans: Pro subscription

### 2. **Personalized Product Grading**
- A-F grades for every product
- Based on user's skin profile
- Explains WHY a product is good/bad for you
- Shows ingredient safety alerts

### 3. **Multi-Region Marketplace**
- Auto-detects India vs Global
- INR/USD pricing
- 19 curated products (Minimalist, CeraVe, The Ordinary, etc.)
- Affiliate links for each product

### 4. **Daily Routine Builder** (Pro)
- AM/PM skincare routines
- Personalized to your skin type
- Diet recommendations for skin health

### 5. **Authentication System**
- Google OAuth + Email/Password
- Secure session management
- Profile with skin data persistence

### 6. **Content Hub**
- `/blog` — 4 skincare articles with full markdown
- `/about` — Company mission & stats
- `/privacy` & `/terms` — Legal pages
